Tobey Maguire in Avengers: Doomsday? Spider-Man vs X-Men Rumors Explained

Updated November 2026 · Marvel · Spider-Man · Avengers: Doomsday
Rumor update: Tobey Maguire's return as Spider-Man in Avengers: Doomsday has been widely rumored, but Marvel Studios has not officially confirmed that he is in the movie.

Could Tobey Maguire return as Spider-Man in Avengers: Doomsday and find himself fighting the X-Men? That question has become one of the biggest mysteries surrounding Marvel's next massive multiverse crossover.

Rumors surrounding Maguire's possible return have continued to circulate, with some reports claiming that his version of Peter Parker could become involved in a major multiversal conflict involving the X-Men, Wolverine and other legacy Marvel characters.

Nothing about Tobey Maguire's role has been officially confirmed by Marvel Studios. However, the combination of the returning X-Men, the multiverse storyline and Maguire's history with Marvel has made the rumor particularly interesting for fans.

Is Tobey Maguire in Avengers: Doomsday?

The short answer is: it has not been officially confirmed.

Marvel Studios' official cast announcements for Avengers: Doomsday include a huge lineup of Marvel characters, including members of the original Fox-era X-Men, the Avengers, the Fantastic Four and other heroes.

Tobey Maguire, however, has not been officially announced as part of the cast. That means claims describing his appearance as confirmed should still be treated carefully.

At the same time, several reports and insider claims have continued to link Maguire to the movie. One recent report even describes a possible Raimi-era Spider-Man sequence connected to the X-Men. 2

Why Are Fans Talking About Tobey Maguire and the X-Men?

The biggest reason is the story Marvel has officially revealed for Avengers: Doomsday.

Marvel has confirmed that the movie brings together heroes from three different universes. The film also features several returning actors from the original X-Men movies, including Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen, James Marsden, Rebecca Romijn, Alan Cumming, Kelsey Grammer and Channing Tatum.

That makes the idea of Tobey Maguire's Spider-Man crossing into the X-Men universe much easier to imagine than it would have been in a normal standalone Marvel movie.

The entire premise of Avengers: Doomsday is built around different Marvel worlds colliding, making almost anything possible within the multiverse.

Tobey Maguire's Spider-Man vs Wolverine

One of the most talked-about rumors is a potential confrontation between Tobey Maguire's Spider-Man and Hugh Jackman's Wolverine.

The idea is particularly exciting because both characters represent different eras of Marvel's live-action history.

Maguire's Spider-Man became one of the defining superhero characters of the early 2000s, while Jackman's Wolverine became the face of the original X-Men movie franchise.

Some reports have claimed that the two characters could actually fight each other in Avengers: Doomsday. However, this remains a rumor and has not been confirmed by Marvel Studios. 3

Spider-Man vs Wolverine: Who Would Win?

If the rumored fight actually happens, it could become one of the most unexpected Marvel matchups ever put on screen.

Spider-Man has speed, agility, spider-sense and web-shooters, while Wolverine has an adamantium skeleton, enhanced healing and retractable claws.

The biggest question would not simply be who is stronger. It would be whether the two heroes are actually enemies or are forced to fight because of the multiverse conflict.

Could Spider-Man Fight the X-Men?

Another version of the rumor suggests something even bigger: Tobey Maguire's Spider-Man could find himself caught in a conflict between his universe and the Fox-era X-Men.

Reports have circulated about a possible opening sequence involving Maguire's Spider-Man and X-Men characters. Other versions of the rumor describe an incursion between the world of Sam Raimi's Spider-Man trilogy and the X-Men universe.

These details should not be treated as confirmed plot information. Different versions of the rumor have circulated online, and some alleged leaked footage and images have also been questioned or described as unverified.

Still, the idea fits the basic concept Marvel has officially established: multiple universes are heading toward a collision in Avengers: Doomsday. 4

What About Doctor Doom?

At the center of all this chaos is Victor von Doom, played by Robert Downey Jr.

Marvel has officially positioned Doctor Doom as one of the central figures in the film, while the story involves an existential threat to the future of the multiverse.

If Tobey Maguire really is part of the movie, his Spider-Man would therefore be entering a much larger conflict than simply another superhero battle.

His universe could become one of the many realities caught in the multiversal crisis created by Doom.

Why Tobey Maguire's Return Would Be a Big Deal

Tobey Maguire already made his historic return as Peter Parker in Spider-Man: No Way Home, where he shared the screen with Tom Holland and Andrew Garfield.

That appearance proved that Marvel and Sony were willing to bring different Spider-Man generations together.

Now, Avengers: Doomsday is taking the multiverse concept to an even larger scale by bringing together the Avengers, Fantastic Four and the original X-Men.

Marvel Studios also used archival footage of Maguire's Spider-Man in its 2026 Comic-Con presentation, which reignited speculation about a possible return. Importantly, that footage was not an official confirmation that Maguire filmed new scenes for Doomsday. 5

Avengers: Doomsday Release Date

Avengers: Doomsday is scheduled to arrive in cinemas in December 2026.

Disney's UK release information lists the film for December 18, 2026, while Disney has separately announced that the movie will reach UK cinemas earlier, on December 16, 2026. 6

The film is directed by Anthony Russo and Joe Russo, with Robert Downey Jr. returning to Marvel in the role of Doctor Doom.

So, Will Tobey Maguire Really Return?

For now, the safest answer is: we don't know yet.

There is enough speculation to make Tobey Maguire's possible return one of the most interesting unanswered questions surrounding Avengers: Doomsday, but there is still no official Marvel confirmation.

The X-Men connection makes the rumor even more exciting. If Maguire's Spider-Man really does cross paths with Wolverine, Beast, Cyclops or other X-Men characters, Marvel could deliver one of the most unexpected superhero encounters in movie history.

Until Marvel officially reveals the truth, fans will have to wait and see whether the original big-screen Spider-Man swings back into the Multiverse Saga.
