What Is The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping About?

Sunrise on the Reaping takes place 24 years before the events of the original The Hunger Games.

The story begins on the morning of the Reaping for the 50th Hunger Games, also known as the Second Quarter Quell.

The special rules of the Quarter Quell mean that 48 tributes are selected instead of the usual 24.

One of those tributes is 16-year-old Haymitch Abernathy from District 12.

Who Is Young Haymitch Abernathy?

Haymitch Abernathy is one of the most important characters in The Hunger Games universe.

In the original movies, Woody Harrelson portrayed Haymitch as the mentor who helped Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark survive their Hunger Games.

Sunrise on the Reaping goes back to Haymitch's teenage years and shows the experience that eventually shaped the character audiences know from the original trilogy.

Are Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson and Woody Harrelson Returning?

Yes. Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson and Woody Harrelson appear in the new movie as returning characters from the original Hunger Games films.

Their appearances connect the new prequel with the original franchise, while Joseph Zada plays the younger Haymitch Abernathy at the center of the new story.

What Is The Second Quarter Quell?

The Second Quarter Quell is the 50th Hunger Games and is one of the most significant events in the history of Panem.

For this special edition of the Games, the Capitol doubles the number of tributes.

Instead of 24 competitors, 48 tributes are forced into the arena.

Haymitch becomes one of those tributes, beginning one of the darkest periods of his life.

Who Plays President Snow?

Ralph Fiennes plays President Coriolanus Snow in the new movie.

The film explores the Capitol's control over the Hunger Games, propaganda and the political power surrounding the arena.

Will Sunrise on the Reaping Be Released in IMAX?

Yes. The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is scheduled for theatrical and IMAX release.

The large-format release makes the return to the Hunger Games arena an especially significant cinematic event for fans of the franchise.
