2. Story and Synopsis: What Is the Movie About?

Sunrise on the Reaping takes place 24 years before the first The Hunger Games film. It begins on the morning of the reaping for the 50th annual Hunger Games, a special edition called the Second Quarter Quell.

Sixteen-year-old Haymitch Abernathy, from District 12, is chosen to compete in the Capitol's deadly spectacle. This Quarter Quell has a brutal rule: twice the usual number of tributes are sent into the arena. Instead of 24 competitors, 48 young people are forced to fight for survival.

Inside the arena, Haymitch forms an unexpected alliance with fellow tribute Maysilee Donner. As the Games progress, he becomes involved in a wider effort to challenge the Capitol's control under President Coriolanus Snow. The story explores the choices, losses and resistance that help shape the person Haymitch will become.

The movie is based on Suzanne Collins' novel of the same name. This is a prequel, so its central events happen long before Katniss Everdeen volunteers for her sister in the 74th Hunger Games.

3. Who Is Young Haymitch Abernathy?

Haymitch Abernathy is known to franchise audiences as the sharp-witted, often cynical mentor who guides Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark. In the original films, the adult Haymitch was played by Woody Harrelson.

In this prequel, Joseph Zada portrays Haymitch at age 16. The film explores the traumatic experience of the 50th Hunger Games and the events that help explain his later distrust of the Capitol, his complicated personality and his approach to survival.

His story is not simply about winning an arena competition. It is also about loyalty, loss, strategy and the cost of resisting an authoritarian system.
