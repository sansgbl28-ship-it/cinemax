The Cat in the Hat

2026 Animation Adventure Comedy Family 107 min

The Cat in the Hat (2026) is a brand-new animated theatrical adventure based on the iconic Dr. Seuss character. The film stars Bill Hader as the voice of The Cat and follows his unusual mission to help siblings Gabby and Sebastian after they move to a new town.

Everything to Know About The Cat in the Hat (2026)

The Cat in the Hat returns to the big screen in a completely new animated adventure from Warner Bros. Pictures Animation.

Instead of simply recreating the short 1957 children's book, the filmmakers have expanded the world of the Cat into a feature-length story with new characters, new locations and a deeper look at the Cat himself.

In this version, the Cat works for the mysterious I.I.I.I. — Institute for the Institution of Imagination and Inspiration, LLC.

His newest assignment is to cheer up Gabby and Sebastian, two siblings struggling to adjust after moving to a new town.

But the Cat has a habit of taking things much further than expected. What begins as a simple mission quickly turns into a chaotic and magical adventure.

Story & Synopsis

The Cat in the Hat has an unusual profession. He works as an agent for the I.I.I.I., an organization whose mission is to bring imagination and inspiration into the lives of children.

For his latest assignment, the Cat must help Gabby and Sebastian, siblings who are having trouble adapting to their new life after moving to another town.

The goal is simple: make the children happy again.

The execution is anything but simple.

The Cat's unpredictable personality and tendency to take things too far quickly turn the assignment into a much larger adventure. As the situation becomes increasingly chaotic, the Cat realizes that this may be his final chance to prove himself.

If he fails, he could lose his position with the I.I.I.I. — and even his magical hat.

What Makes The Cat in the Hat (2026) Different?

The 2026 movie is not simply a scene-for-scene recreation of the original 1957 book.

The filmmakers have expanded the original concept into a feature-length adventure with new characters, new settings and a much larger story.

The movie also explores more of the Cat's personality and his unusual role as an agent for the I.I.I.I.

This makes the film a new animated interpretation of the Dr. Seuss character rather than a remake of the 2003 live-action movie.

Director & Production

The movie is directed by Erica Rivinoja and Alessandro Carloni, who also wrote the screenplay. The screen story comes from Caroline Williams and the film is based on the book by Dr. Seuss.

The producers are Daniela Mazzucato and Jared Stern. Music is by Lorne Balfe and editing is by Dan Molina.

The Cat in the Hat is also notable as Warner Bros. Pictures Animation's first full-length feature film.

The Cat in the Hat and Dr. Seuss

The original The Cat in the Hat children's book was written and illustrated by Dr. Seuss and first published in 1957.

The famous character became one of Dr. Seuss's most recognizable creations, known for his red-and-white striped hat, mischievous personality and unpredictable adventures.

The 2026 movie takes that familiar character and expands his world into a full-length animated theatrical adventure.
