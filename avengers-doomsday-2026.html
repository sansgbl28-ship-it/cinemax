Avengers: Doomsday – Everything You Need to Know

Marvel Studios is preparing one of its biggest cinematic events yet with Avengers: Doomsday. The upcoming superhero movie brings together characters from different corners of the Marvel universe and places them on a collision course with an extraordinary threat.

But what exactly is happening in Avengers: Doomsday? Why is Robert Downey Jr. returning to Marvel as Doctor Doom? And how will the Avengers, Fantastic Four and X-Men fit into the same story?

Here is everything currently known about the movie, including its UK release date, cast, story, Doctor Doom, the multiverse connection and the official trailer.

What Is Avengers: Doomsday About?

The exact plot of Avengers: Doomsday is being kept tightly under wraps, but Marvel has confirmed the central idea behind the movie.

Heroes from three distinct universes are placed on a dangerous collision course as they face an existential threat unlike anything they have encountered before.

That setup makes Doomsday more than another conventional Avengers adventure. The story is expected to bring together heroes and characters who previously existed in very different corners of Marvel's cinematic history.

The result could be one of the largest crossovers in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Why Is Doctor Doom So Important?

One of the biggest questions surrounding Avengers: Doomsday is the role of Victor von Doom, better known as Doctor Doom.

Robert Downey Jr. returns to the Marvel universe in this new role after spending years associated with Tony Stark and Iron Man.

The casting immediately created one of the biggest mysteries surrounding the film: How will Robert Downey Jr.'s new Marvel character connect to the wider multiverse?

Marvel has not revealed every detail of Doctor Doom's plan, which gives the character a major mystery heading into the film's theatrical release.

Who Directed Avengers: Doomsday?

Anthony Russo and Joe Russo direct Avengers: Doomsday. The Russo brothers previously directed major Marvel films including Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame.

The screenplay is credited to Stephen McFeely, another key creative figure from Marvel's previous Avengers era.

How Does Avengers: Doomsday Connect to the Multiverse?

The multiverse is central to the mystery surrounding Avengers: Doomsday. Marvel has confirmed that heroes from three distinct universes are involved in the story.

That opens the door for characters from different Marvel eras and franchises to appear together in a single conflict.

For fans, one of the biggest attractions is the possibility of seeing characters who previously seemed separated by different timelines, universes or movie franchises finally share the same story.

Are the Fantastic Four in Avengers: Doomsday?

Yes. The film includes the Fantastic Four, with Pedro Pascal as Reed Richards, Vanessa Kirby as Sue Storm, Joseph Quinn as Johnny Storm and Ebon Moss-Bachrach as Ben Grimm.

Their involvement adds another major dimension to the story because the Fantastic Four are closely connected to the larger Marvel multiverse storyline.

Are the X-Men in Avengers: Doomsday?

The cast also includes several actors associated with the X-Men universe, including Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen, James Marsden, Rebecca Romijn and Alan Cumming.

Their presence is one of the reasons Avengers: Doomsday has generated so much interest among longtime Marvel fans.

What Could Happen in Avengers: Doomsday?

Although Marvel is keeping major story details secret, the confirmed multiverse setup creates several enormous questions.

Will the Avengers be able to trust heroes from completely different universes? Why is Doctor Doom bringing these characters together? And what happens when the boundaries between Marvel's different realities begin to collapse?

Is Avengers: Doomsday Connected to Avengers: Secret Wars?

Avengers: Doomsday is part of Marvel's larger Multiverse Saga and is expected to play an important role in setting up the events that follow.

That makes Doomsday particularly important for fans who want to understand where the Marvel Cinematic Universe is heading next.
