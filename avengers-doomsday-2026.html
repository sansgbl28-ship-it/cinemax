Movie Overview

In Avengers: Doomsday, heroes from different universes are brought together to confront a dangerous new threat. With Robert Downey Jr. taking on the role of Victor von Doom, the Marvel Cinematic Universe enters a dramatic new chapter. The film is directed by Anthony Russo and Joe Russo and written by Stephen McFeely.
